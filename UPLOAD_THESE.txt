Customer master Excel — location optional, set GPS later
========================================================

- Bulk Excel upload no longer needs GPS/location columns filled.
- Download "Excel template" from Customers tab (location column blank).
- After import, use "Set GPS" / "Edit GPS" on each customer row.
- Paste Google Maps link OR lat,lng (e.g. 6.9271, 79.8612).
- Type CLEAR in the GPS prompt to remove coordinates.
