Bank-details invite link (copy / WhatsApp)
==========================================

When a customer is payout-eligible but has no bank details:
  Finance or Admin can click "Request bank details"
  → generates a 7-day secure link on the claim portal
  → Copy to clipboard, or type WA to open WhatsApp with message

Customer opens:
  https://rewards.cloudebase.dpdns.org/bank.html?token=...

Requires CLAIM_PORTAL_URL in admin/config.js (already set).

New file: public/bank.html
